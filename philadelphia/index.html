How to check 311 complaints and violations for a Philadelphia address

You research the house and inherit the street. A listing tells you about the property; the city's record tells you about the block around it.

Philadelphia publishes its civic data through a public API at phl.carto.com. That means anyone can look up what has been reported near an address — you don't need an account, and you don't need to know SQL. Agent Neighbor does the query for you and reads it live in your browser.

  1. Open the address lookup and type a Philadelphia address, or drop a pin on the map.
  2. The report reads the city's 311, violation and permit feeds for the block and pulls the trailing twelve months of records.
  3. You get one Street Context Index plus the individual records behind it — each with its date and a link to the original city record, so you can verify anything that matters.

What the record can tell a buyer

A cluster of sanitation or illegal-dumping complaints reads differently from a single noise report. A run of building permits nearby can mean a block on the way up — or years of construction next door. Repeat L&I violations at one property are worth a question before you make an offer. None of this replaces an inspection; it tells you what to look into.

Neighborhoods this covers

The lookup works for any address inside the city — Center City, Fishtown, South Philly, West Philly, Germantown, the Northeast and everywhere between — because it reads the same citywide feeds rather than a fixed list of neighborhoods.

Frequently asked

How recent is the data?

Reports cover the trailing twelve months and refresh nightly from the city's feed. Philadelphia publishes on its own schedule, so a complaint filed this morning may take a day or two to appear.

Is this an official city tool?

No. Agent Neighbor is an independent product that reads Philadelphia's public open data. Scores are for informational purposes only — not an appraisal, an inspection, or a safety guarantee. Always verify anything that matters with the original source.
