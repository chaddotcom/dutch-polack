How it works

Three steps, no interpretation required.

Pin the street

We resolve your address to a street segment, not a zip code and not a census tract. Two blocks apart can score twenty points apart, and that difference is the entire point.

Pull the record

Every 311 call, code violation, permit and incident filed within range over the last twelve months, straight from the city's own public data feeds.

Read the score

Six domains roll into one index from 0 to 100. Open any domain and you see the records underneath it, dated and attributed. Nothing is a black box.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Where does the data come from?

Municipal open data programs, published by the cities themselves. Philadelphia serves 311 requests, Licenses and Inspections violations, and building permits. New York serves 311, HPD housing violations, and DOB permits. Every record in a report carries its date and its source, and you can go read the original.

Is this about my future neighbors?

No, and deliberately so. Agent Neighbor scores civic activity at a place. It does not profile residents, it does not touch demographics, and it does not surface anything about individuals. That is an ethical line and also a practical one, since agents cannot legally steer clients on those grounds.

Which cities work right now?

Philadelphia and New York are first, because both publish clean, coordinate-tagged civic data through free public APIs. Austin, Boston, Seattle, San Francisco, Chicago, Washington, Denver and Portland follow. Anywhere outside a covered city currently returns demonstration data, clearly labeled.

What does the score actually mean?

The index runs 0 to 100, where higher means a quieter civic record. It is a weighted roll-up of six domains, and it is a starting point for questions rather than a verdict. A low score on a street you love means go ask about the permits, not walk away.

How current is it?

Records refresh nightly from each city's feed, and a report covers the trailing twelve months. Cities publish on their own schedules, so a complaint filed this morning may take a day or two to appear.

Can I use these reports with clients?

Yes. The Agent and Team plan produces co-branded reports built entirely from public records with sources attached, which is what makes them safe to hand to a buyer.
